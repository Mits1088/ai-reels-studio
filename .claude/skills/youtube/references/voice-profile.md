# Creator Voice Profile

This reference captures the durable style and delivery principles for this creator. Load this at Phase Y1 before writing the script. These rules apply regardless of topic.

---

## Style Principles (Permanent)

### Register
- Conversational, direct, personal
- Heavy use of "you" — make it about the viewer, not abstract
- Never formal, academic, or corporate in tone
- Comfortable with rhetorical questions

### Sentence structure
- Short punchy sentences between longer explanations for rhythm
- "Simple idea." / "That's the real shift." / "Done." — one-word or two-word sentences land hard after a setup
- Spoken rhythm takes priority over grammatically correct writing
- Natural spoken phrasing: "By the end of this video, in the next twelve minutes" — adds a pause/clarification that wouldn't be written that way but sounds right spoken

### Phrasing patterns
- Simple verb forms: "what one does" not "what one is" / "what it actually cost" not "what it actually costs"
- More direct: "way more affordable than you think" not "way more affordable than you'd expect"
- Drop unnecessary words: "answers questions" not "answers customer questions"
- Natural starters: "Let me show you" / "Here's the thing" / "Here's what I mean" / "Most people"
- Active phrasing: "Anthropic just removed all of that" not "Anthropic has eliminated the infrastructure layer"
- "Most people" not "most teams" — more inclusive, less corporate

### Scenario framing
- Put the viewer in the situation with "Imagine you have..." / "Imagine you closed..."
- Frame concepts by what they replace, not by defining them abstractly
- Explain what would have been required before — contrast is the explanation

## Vocabulary Check (Per Topic — Derived from Demo Ingest)

Jargon should only be used if the creator naturally uses it during the demo-ingest voice recording. When in doubt, simplify.

**Process:** During Phase Y0 (demo-ingest), note which technical terms the creator uses naturally in their spoken notes. Those terms are in-scope. Terms they avoid or paraphrase around are out of scope for the script.

**Examples from the Claude Managed Agents video:**
- "observability" → replace with "you need to see exactly what the agent did and why it made the decisions it made"
- "scaffolding" → replace with a plain description of what the scaffolding actually does
- "harness" → replace with a plain description

Do not carry these specific replacements into future videos unless the same pattern appears in that video's demo-ingest notes.

---

## What Not to Do

- Do not open with "Hey guys" or any generic greeting
- Do not define a term before showing what it does — show it, then name it
- Do not write passive voice: "it was discovered that" → "they found"
- Do not use "leverage" as a verb
- Do not write sentences that would sound stiff when said out loud
- Do not describe what the viewer can already see on screen — explain why it matters or what it replaces
