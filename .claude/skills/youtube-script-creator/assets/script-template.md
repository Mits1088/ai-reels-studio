## Full script

### Hook (0:00)

[Open with the problem, not the product. 3 to 4 sentences naming the hidden complexity. End with "Anthropic / [company] just changed that. And in this video, I'll show you exactly what you can build."]

### Intro and promise (0:30)

[If you... this video is for you. By the end of the next twelve minutes, you'll [three specific things]. I'm not going to [set expectation about what's not in the video]. Let's go straight in.]

### The problem (1:30)

[Two practical scenarios from different business contexts. Infrastructure list or pain list. Proof point from a real company with specific numbers.]

### What changed (3:30)

[One paragraph. Plain English. What [company] built. How it changes the game. Transition: "Let me show you."]

*Mid-video CTA here (right before the first demo chapter): "Actually, quick pause. If you're finding this useful, hit subscribe. I break down AI tools every week for people who want to actually use them. More like this in your feed."*

### Platform orientation (4:00)

[Quick tour of the interface. Point and name each section. No clicks yet. End with "That's the lay of the land. Now let's build something."]

### First demo (4:45)

**Phase A: Setup (before action)**
[What we're about to do, why it matters.]

**Phase B: Void-filler (during action)**
[While this loads, here's what's happening behind the scenes... Contrast with old way... bridge to next step.]

**Phase C: Review (after action)**
[How good is that. Let me just check what it did. Point out one thing that works, one to watch.]

**Prompts used in this demo:**
- `[TYPE: exact prompt 1]`
- `[TYPE: exact prompt 2]`

### Deeper capability (6:00)

[Same three-phase structure for the next demo step.]

### Behind the scenes (7:30)

[Show something the viewer can't see from the demo alone. Audit log, session view, vault, etc. This is the "deployable in real environments" beat.]

### Extended demo or second example (8:45)

*Retention re-hook here (right before this chapter): "Now here's the part most videos on this topic skip over, and honestly it's the reason this is actually useful in real business environments..."*

[Full demo of the second example, using the three-phase structure. Reinforce with a different angle from the first demo.]

### Pricing (9:30)

[Lead with the number. Explain what's charged. Contrast with enterprise AI tool pricing. Give the smallest possible entry point.]

### Outro (10:15)

[So here's what changed: [one-sentence core shift]. [The specific thing from this video] used to take [old cost]. Now it's [new cost]. If you want to try it, [URL] is in the description. Subscribe if this was useful. Next up, I'm covering [next video], [one sentence tease].]
