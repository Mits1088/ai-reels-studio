Neural networks in plain English:

Think of a neural network like a team of people passing notes.

  • Each person (neuron) reads a note, decides if it's important, and passes a new note on
  • The team learns by adjusting how much attention they pay to each note
  • After millions of practice rounds, they get very good at recognising patterns

That's it — layers of simple decisions stacked together create surprisingly intelligent behaviour.
