# Reel Brief

## Project
- **Project name**: example-project
- **Content type**: demo
- **Working title**: Deploy in One Click — Product Demo

## Goal
Show developers they can deploy in one click and drive them to try the free tier.

## Audience
Developers and technical product managers who deploy web apps regularly.

## Reel Target
- **Platform**: Instagram Reels
- **Format**: 1080x1920
- **Target duration**: ~15s
- **CTA**: Try it free — link in bio

## Core Message
One-click deploy eliminates pipelines and waiting.

## Support Points
1. No pipelines required
2. No waiting — instant deploy
3. Visual proof of the deploy button in action

## Hook Options
1. "What if you could deploy in one click?" (question hook)
2. "Stop waiting for your deploy pipeline" (pain point hook)
3. "One button. That's it." (minimal hook)

## Visual Direction
- **avatar**: Full-screen for hook (beat-01) and CTA (beat-04), hidden during demo
- **demo footage**: Deploy button screenshot, cropped for clarity (beat-03)
- **support visuals**: Success animation overlay during CTA (beat-04)
- **captions**: Every beat captioned, mobile-safe positioning
- **transitions**: Single fade into demo beat, cuts elsewhere
- **SFX**: Whoosh on button click (beat-03)

## Constraints
- No extra visuals beyond what the story needs
- Keep under 15 seconds
- Music volume must stay below voice

## Success Criteria
The reel is successful if:
- The deploy action is visually clear in under 4 seconds of screen time
- The CTA feels natural, not forced
- Viewer understands the value proposition without reading a description
